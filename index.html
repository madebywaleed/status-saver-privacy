Status Saver – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Status Saver (the "App") is an Android app published by Khan Labs Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App does with information. If you have questions, contact us at khanlabs@waleedkhan.agency.

1. The short version

  • The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos and videos stay on your device. We do not upload them to any server.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information (see section 5).

2. What the App does

Status Saver lets you view and save photos and videos from the status folder of WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business that is stored on your own phone. To do this, the App asks you to choose that folder using Android's standard folder picker. The App can only read the folder you select.

3. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following only on your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the App:

  • Your settings (for example, which app you selected and whether auto-save is on).
  • The folder permission you granted.
  • A list of the statuses you saved, so the Saved screen can show them.

Statuses you save are placed in your phone's gallery. They stay there after you uninstall the App unless you delete them yourself.

4. Information we receive

We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. When the App starts, it downloads a small settings file (folder locations and file types) from GitHub. As with any internet request, the host of that file can see your IP address and basic technical information such as your device's browser or app identifier. No personal information from your phone is sent with this request.

5. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions in order to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In some regions (such as the European Economic Area and the UK) the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. Purchases

If we offer an optional upgrade (for example, removing ads), the purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

7. Permissions the App uses

  • Folder access (chosen by you): to read WhatsApp statuses from the folder you select.
  • Photos and media: to save statuses you choose to your gallery.
  • Internet: to load ads and the settings file.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Not affiliated with WhatsApp

Status Saver is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta Platforms, Inc. "WhatsApp" is a trademark of its owner. Statuses belong to the people who created them. Please respect their rights and save, share or repost content only when you have permission.

10. Your choices

You can stop the App from reading statuses at any time by changing the folder permission in the App's Settings or by uninstalling the App. You can delete saved items from the Saved screen or your gallery.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
